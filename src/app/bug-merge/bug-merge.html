A WHOLE LITTLE EVOLUTION

From tiny speck
to garden royalty.

Every merge reveals a larger bug with a new symbol and personality. Can you discover the legendary 2048 bug?

  1. Slide the whole garden.Use the arrow keys, WASD, a swipe or the direction buttons. All bugs move together.
  2. Match equal numbers.Two 8 bugs become a 16 bug. Each bug merges once per move. A new 2 or 4 arrives after every valid move.
  3. Keep some breathing room.Plan ahead. A full garden with no matching neighbors ends your run. Your highest score stays on this device.
