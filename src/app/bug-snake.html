SMALL BUG. LONG JOURNEY.

One more bite.
One longer bug.

Your bug grows a new body segment with every pink flower it eats. Aim for your longest bug and your longest run.

  1. Follow your appetite.Find the pink flower. Every snack adds one segment.
  2. Leave yourself room.Avoid the edges and your own body. You cannot reverse into your neck.
  3. Stay a little longer.No countdown. A steady pace, a growing challenge and a personal best saved on this device.

HOW TO STEER

Move the mouse toward the direction you want to go, or focus the board and use ↑ ← ↓ → / WASD. On touch screens, drag on the board or use the buttons below.

P, Space or Esc pauses. Switching tabs pauses automatically.
